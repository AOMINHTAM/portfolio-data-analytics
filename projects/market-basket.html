ASSOCIATION RULES • CASE STUDY

Market Basket Analysis & Product Bundling Strategy

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, mlxtend (Apriori), Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn Focus: Association Rules Mining, Support/Confidence/Lift Optimization

1. Business Context & Strategic Objectives

The e-commerce platform sought to increase Average Order Value (AOV) by replacing generic cross-selling prompts with algorithmic association rules mining.

  • Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on basket size distributions and top-moving categories.
  • Apply the Apriori Algorithm to compute Support, Confidence, and Lift for co-occurring product combinations.
  • Identify cohesive "Shopping Ecosystems" to engineer high-margin product bundles (e.g., Weekend Reset Kit).

2. Key Findings & Behavioral Affinity Rules

  • Highest Lift Linkage:
    [Candles, Room Sprays, Face Masks] → [Nail Care, Bath Oils/Bombs]
    • Lift: 4.25 (Customers purchasing the antecedent are 4.25x more likely to buy the consequent compared to random baseline).
    • Confidence: 45% of buyers adding Candles and Masks actively add Nail Care or Bath Oils to finalize their cart.
  • Shopping Ecosystem A ("Home Spa"): A self-care basket containing Candles + Face Masks + Bath Oils + Nail Care purchased to simulate an at-home spa experience.
  • Shopping Ecosystem B ("Beauty from Within"): A wellness-driven basket combining Vitamins/Supplements + Hand Creams + Face Care, representing a disciplined health-conscious demographic.

3. Technical Debt Audited & Bug Fixes

  • Out-of-Memory (OOM) Prevention via Support Tuning: Initial `min_support` of `0.001` (0.1%) generated millions of combinatorial itemsets, crashing kernel RAM. Recalibrated threshold to `0.005` (0.5%) to maintain compute stability without losing high-lift rules.
  • In-Basket Deduplication: Refactored raw item aggregation using .unique(), eliminating repeated subcategories within a single `OrderID` prior to `TransactionEncoder` processing.
  • Pre-Algorithmic EDA Pipeline: Engineered exploratory subplots visualizing top-10 category velocity and basket-size distributions before model execution.

4. Actionable Commercial Recommendations

  • Launch the "Weekend Reset Kit": Package Ecosystem A items together with a 10% bundle discount, increasing Average Order Value.
  • Dynamic Checkout Cross-Selling: When a user adds Candles to cart, trigger a dynamic prompt suggesting Bath Bombs instead of competing candle brands.
  • Targeted Content Marketing: Direct the creative team to produce short-form video content titled "The 4-Step Post-Deadline Self-Care Routine" featuring the Ecosystem A product combination.
