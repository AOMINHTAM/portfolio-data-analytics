TeddyWorld — Revenue Contribution Analysis via Pareto (80/20)

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Dataset: 32,000+ E-commerce Orders

1. Business Context & Objectives

TeddyWorld retails a wide catalog of plush toys and gift accessories. Management needed to assess portfolio revenue concentration to optimize marketing budget allocation and warehouse stock policies.

  • Calculate accurate Net Revenue post-refunds (Net Revenue = Gross Revenue - Refunds).
  • Determine cumulative revenue contribution per SKU using descending running sums.
  • Segment products into Pareto thresholds: Top 80% Core vs. Bottom 20% Tail.
  • Deliver actionable strategic recommendations for inventory management and digital ad spend.

2. Key Findings & Cumulative Contribution

The Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule) was fully validated: just 3 core SKUs account for ~81.9% of total net revenue.

Rank Product Name Units Sold Net Revenue Share (%) Cumulative (%) Pareto Segment
1 The Original Mr. Fuzzy 15,200 $485,000 45.2% 45.2% Top 80% (Core)
2 Forever Bear 8,400 $268,800 25.1% 70.3% Top 80% (Core)
3 The Birthday Sugar Panda 3,900 $124,800 11.6% 81.9% Top 80% (Core)
4 The Hudson River Mini bear 3,100 $99,200 9.3% 91.2% Bottom 20% (Tail)

3. Business Insights & Engineering Notes

  • Single SKU Vulnerability: Over 45% of net revenue depends entirely on The Original Mr. Fuzzy. Any supply chain bottleneck on this SKU directly disrupts cash flow.
  • Underperforming Long Tail: Low-velocity accessories consume valuable warehouse space while offering negligible contribution margins.
  • Technical Guardrail: Refund records must be subtracted prior to grouping to avoid gross revenue inflation. Strict descending sorting (`sort_values(ascending=False)`) is mandated before `.cumsum()`.

4. Actionable Strategic Recommendations

  • Prioritized Inventory Buffer: Enforce strict safety stocks and lead-time tracking for the Top 3 SKUs to avoid peak-season stockouts.
  • Product Bundling: Pair slow-moving accessories with top-performing plush toys to liquidate stagnant inventory.
  • Digital Marketing Reallocation: Channel 70–80% of acquisition ad budgets into the Top 3 conversion champions to maximize ROAS.
