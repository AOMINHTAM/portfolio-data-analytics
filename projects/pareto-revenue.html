TeddyWorld — Revenue Contribution Analysis via Pareto (80/20)

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Dual-Axis Visualizations Scale: 32,000+ E-Commerce Orders / Multi-SKU Portfolio

1. Business Scenario & Net Revenue Concentration

Applied the Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule) to quantify revenue concentration post-refund deductions, identifying core revenue drivers and supply chain risks across TeddyWorld's product catalog.

2. Key Findings & Pareto Classification

  • Hyper-Concentration: Only 3 core SKUs generate ~82% of top-line company net revenue.
  • Single-Point-of-Failure Risk: A single product (The Original Mr. Fuzzy) accounts for >45% of gross cash flow, exposing the business to inventory stockout shocks.

3. Actionable Supply Chain Recommendations

  • Dynamic Safety Stock: Established dynamic safety stock quotas prioritizing the top 3 SKUs to avoid cash flow bottlenecks.
  • Long-Tail Rationalization: Optimized inventory holdings on low-margin products contributing <5% of cumulative sales.
