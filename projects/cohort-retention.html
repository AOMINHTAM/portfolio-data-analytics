BEHAVIORAL ANALYTICS • CASE STUDY

Cohort Analysis & Customer Retention Dynamics

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn Focus: Time-Based Cohorts, Churn Auditing, B2B/B2C Segmentation

1. Business Scenario & The "Leaky Bucket" Problem

Despite steady top-of-funnel acquisition, the business suffered unpredictable top-line revenue fluctuations. The analytics team set out to investigate a severe "leaky bucket" phenomenon where newly acquired customers churned prematurely before returning positive ROI.

  • Build a granular Time-Based Cohort model (Acquisition Month vs. Elapsed Months).
  • Segment retention performance to identify structural differences between Corporate (B2B) and Consumer (B2C) units.
  • Evaluate the Scale vs. Efficiency trade-off across customer income brackets.
  • Audit and resolve data engineering traps including left-truncation bias in cohort tracking.

2. Key Findings & Behavioral Discoveries

  • The Month-1 Churn Crisis: While Q1 acquisition volume was high, Month-1 retention collapsed to a dismal 1% to 3% baseline across all cohorts, proving a complete lack of post-purchase onboarding engagement.
  • Corporate (B2B) Restock Cyclicality: Unlike erratic B2C buying, B2B corporate buyers exhibited clear retention spikes (+5% to 6%) at Month 5 and Month 8, reflecting structural inventory restock rhythms.
  • The Income Profitability Paradox:
    • Low-Income Cohort (The Cash Cow): Generated lowest AOV per transaction, but their high volume and consistency produced the largest cumulative profit pool.
    • High-Income Cohort (The Cash Grab): Highest profit margin per single order, but had the worst repurchase retention rates.

3. Technical Debt Audited & Production Bug Fixes

  • Eliminated Left-Truncation Bias: Identified that computing FirstPurchaseDate *after* subsetting 2022 data falsely misclassified returning 2020–2021 buyers as Month-0 acquisitions. Fixed by anchoring first-purchase dates globally across the unpartitioned master table.
  • Cohort Size Weighting Layout: Paired absolute acquisition volume bar charts side-by-side with retention heatmaps using gridspec_kw={'width_ratios': [1, 4]} to ensure small cohorts were not over-interpreted.
  • Execution Scope Consolidation: Merged disparate plotting cells into a single Object-Oriented pipeline, resolving broken Matplotlib axes across subplots.
  • Consolidated Master Ingestion: Refactored repeated relational joins into an upstream unified df_master table to optimize memory overhead.

4. Strategic Retention Recommendations

  • Automated Post-Purchase Nurture: Launch an automated email re-engagement or "Subscribe & Save" discount sequence triggering at Day 20–25 to stem the Month-1 cliff.
  • B2B Restock CRM Alerts: Schedule automated outreach prompts for sales representatives at Month 4 and Month 7 to capture B2B cyclical reorders.
  • First-Session Cart Upsells: Target High-Income visitors with aggressive first-order cross-sells to maximize revenue capture before churn.
