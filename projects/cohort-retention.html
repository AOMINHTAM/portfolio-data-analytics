Cohort Analysis & Customer Retention Dynamics

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Statistical Auditing Methodology: Time-Based Cohort Matrix & Acquisition Weighting

1. Business Scenario & The Month-1 Retention Cliff

Tracked customer lifecycle retention matrices across acquisition months to audit a steep drop-off between Month 0 and Month 1 (baseline retention of 1.5%–3%).

2. Auditing Left-Truncation Bias (Technical Review)

  • Left-Truncation Bias Rectified: Resolved a critical flaw where first-purchase dates were calculated after filtering for year 2022, erroneously misclassifying 2020–2021 repeat buyers as new 2022 acquisitions.
  • Dual-Panel Layout: Paired acquisition volume bar charts directly with heatmap matrices to prevent over-interpreting percentages from small sample sizes.

3. Commercial Findings & Restock Cycles

  • Cyclical B2B Reorders: Discovered repurchase surges (+5%–6%) occurring systematically at Month 5 and Month 8, reflecting seasonal B2B bulk restocking cycles.
  • Retention Intervention Window: Pinpointed Day 14–21 post-first purchase as the critical window for automated email win-backs.
