CUSTOMER ANALYTICS • CASE STUDY

Customer 360 & RFM Segmentation Analysis

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, NumPy, Squarify, Seaborn, Matplotlib Scope: 51,290 Orders across 17,415 Unique Customers

1. Business Scenario & Strategic Objectives

Marketing leadership at cosmetic retailer CosmeticWorld needed to restructure their annual promotional budget for the New Year Rejuvenation Campaign, shifting from broad-spectrum discounting toward high-ROI customer lifecycle campaigns.

  • Quantify customer behavior across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions using quantile scoring (`pd.qcut`).
  • Classify the customer base into actionable operational cohorts (Champions, Loyal Customers, Cant Lose Them, Hibernating, Lost).
  • Construct a Customer 360 Profile analyzing demographic, geographic, and basket affinities.
  • Suppress unprofitable marketing spend on churned accounts to fund high-touch VIP retention programs.

2. RFM Segment Performance Matrix

The analysis revealed extreme revenue concentration: Champions & Loyal Customers account for under 29% of the customer base but generate over 53% of company revenue ($3.45M+).

3. Customer 360 View: Demographic & Basket Affinities

  • Core Demographics: Peak gross sales (>60%) are driven by ages 25 to 49. High correlation between degree holders (Bachelor’s/Graduate) averaging $60k–$80k income and top Average Order Value (AOV).
  • Geographic Hubs: Asia Pacific and Europe serve as the primary profit engines, outpacing USCA and LATAM markets.
  • Basket Preferences: Champions heavily prioritize premium self-care lines: Nail Care, Hand Creams, and Body Moisturizers.

4. Actionable Campaign Execution

  • 70/30 Budget Reallocation: Allocate 70% of campaign funds exclusively to Champions (concierge gifting) and Cant Lose Them (tailored win-back reactivation vouchers).
  • Direct Mail Suppression: Immediately halt expensive SMS and catalog marketing to Lost and Hibernating cohorts (>5,100 accounts), reducing unnecessary CAC.
  • Cross-Selling Bridge: Package high-performing items (Nail Care & Hand Creams) as Gift-With-Purchase (GWP) rewards for New / Promising buyers to accelerate graduation into Loyal tiers.
