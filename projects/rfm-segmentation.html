CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE • LIFECYCLE ANALYTICS

Customer 360 & Dynamic RFM Segmentation Analysis

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, NumPy, Squarify (Treemap), Seaborn Scale: 51,290 Transactions / 17,415 Unique Accounts

1. Business Scenario & Strategic Problem

CosmeticWorld suffered from significant marketing budget dilution due to blast campaigns sent to non-responsive accounts. Built quantile-based Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) segmentation to allocate CRM budgets effectively.

2. Segment Revenue & Volume Decomposition

Segment Customer Share Revenue Contribution Strategic Roadmap
Champions 13.5% (2,361 accounts) $2.13M (32.8%) VIP loyalty perks & early access
Can't Lose Them 4.8% (833 accounts) $653,354 (10.0%) High-incentive win-back campaigns
Lost / Hibernating 31.2% (5,430+ accounts) 8.2% Suppress paid touchpoints (Save CAC)

3. Demographic Profiling & Budget Reallocation

  • 70% Budget Concentration: Directed 70% of lifecycle retention budgets exclusively toward Champions and Can't Lose Them tiers.
  • Demographic Personas: Professionals aged 25–49 with undergraduate degrees generated over 60% of gross margin.
