Product Performance & Conversion Funnel Analysis

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python, Pandas, Plotly (Funnel), Clickstream Session Diagnostics Scale: 32,000+ Orders / 500,000+ Clickstream Sessions

1. Business Scenario & Drop-off Diagnostics

Tracked customer session progression across critical e-commerce milestones: /products → Product Page → /cart → /shipping → /billing → /thank-you. Pinpointed checkout bottlenecks and identified conversion anomalies across product catalogs.

2. Conversion Funnel Metrics Breakdown

Funnel Stage Page Path Conversion Step Rate Drop-off Rate
Catalog Browse /products 100.0% -
Product View /the-original-mr-fuzzy 74.2% 25.8%
Cart Add /cart 44.8% 55.2%
Billing Drop-off /billing 34.0% 66.0% Drop-off

3. Key Findings & The "Love Bear Paradox"

  • The Love Bear Paradox: Despite receiving only ~10% of total product page views, Love Bear achieved an exceptional 55.6% Cart-Add Rate (vs. 43.1% for Mr. Fuzzy). Recommendation: Promote Love Bear to primary hero banner slots.
  • The 66% Billing Bottleneck: Two-thirds of engaged shoppers dropped off between shipping and billing, indicating UX friction and surprise shipping surcharges.
