Product Performance & Conversion Funnel Analysis

Role: Data Analyst Stack: Python 3.x, Pandas, NumPy, Plotly (Funnel), Seaborn, Matplotlib Scope: Session-Based Clickstream Funnels & Product Net Revenue Auditing

1. Business Scenario & Strategic Objectives

The business was experiencing a significant drop-off between top-of-funnel traffic and completed sales. The analytics team conducted an audit of product-level conversion steps to identify leakages within the browsing-to-purchase journey.

  • Calculate GAAP-aligned Net Revenue after factoring in product refunds across all catalog items.
  • Construct session-based conversion funnels tracking users across milestones: Catalog (/products) → Product Detail Page (PDP) → Cart → Checkout (/thank-you).
  • Compare conversion performance between flagship SKUs: The Original Mr. Fuzzy vs. The Forever Love Bear.

2. Key Findings & The "Love Bear" Paradox

  • The "Love Bear" Paradox:
    • Top-of-Funnel (Catalog → PDP): Mr. Fuzzy captures a massive 62.2% of all catalog traffic, while Love Bear captures only 10.0% due to poor placement.
    • Mid-Funnel (PDP → Cart): Once a user views Love Bear, it generates a significantly higher Cart-Add conversion rate (55.6%) compared to Mr. Fuzzy (43.0%). The product is persuasive but under-exposed.
  • Severe Checkout Bottleneck: Across all product lines, the step from Cart to Completed Order experiences a 66% abandonment rate.
  • Quality Control Red Flag: While Mr. Fuzzy is the primary net revenue driver, The Birthday Sugar Panda exhibits the highest refund rate in the catalog, requiring immediate supplier reviews.

3. Technical QA & Engineering Refactoring

  • Interactive Funnel Architecture: Migrated legacy Matplotlib horizontal bar plots to plotly.graph_objects.Funnel, enabling interactive hover states and step-to-step drop-off ratios.
  • Typecasting Optimization: Replaced repeated datetime casting with a unified ingestion loop utilizing pd.to_datetime().
  • Dependency Deprecation Fixes: Modernized Seaborn plotting parameters (hue='product_name', legend=False) to eliminate deprecation warnings and ensure compatibility with future releases.

4. Actionable Strategic Recommendations

  • Traffic Injection via Cross-Selling: Inject Love Bear as a "Frequently Bought Together" widget directly onto Mr. Fuzzy's high-traffic product page.
  • Checkout UX Overhaul: Audit hidden shipping fees and eliminate mandatory account creation to address the 66% cart abandonment drop-off.
  • Automated Abandoned Cart Flows: Trigger automated email and SMS recovery sequences at 1 hour and 24 hours post-abandonment.
